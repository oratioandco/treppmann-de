Situation

Does this sound familiar?

The constraint is rarely the model or the tool. What is usually missing is a clear priority, a better workflow, and someone who brings leadership, business teams, and IT together.

Licenses everywhere, little use

Copilot or a company GPT has been rolled out, but only a handful of enthusiasts use it. There are no concrete use cases in daily work, no support, and too few leaders going first.

Many ideas, no priority

Every department tries something different. Nobody decides which workflow changes first or how its value will show.

A core process is stuck

Domain knowledge, exceptions, and handoffs do not fit off-the-shelf software. The team needs a better workflow—and a tool shaped around it.

If more than one of these applies, you are not missing technology. You are missing a person who owns it.

The first 90 days

The first 90 days.

Project, interim, or permanent: after 90 days, something is running that people use and whose value can be shown. IT security, legal, data protection, and architecture join as the risk requires—with specialist partners where needed.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Baseline

    Conversations with leadership and business teams, a look at current usage. Outcome: goals and baseline, in writing.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Priorities

    Rate workflows by value, feasibility, and risk. Outcome: three initiatives—and a list of what we deliberately will not do.

  3. Month 2

    Early wins

    Two visible improvements that build trust and show how AI helps in daily work.

  4. Months 2–3

    One process, rebuilt

    Redesign one important workflow with the people who run it, and build it as a working solution.

  5. Day 90

    Anchor

    Named contacts in each team with real time allocated, an AI guideline, training under Art. 4 of the EU AI Act, a measured result—and the next initiative.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Why someone with a product and design background?

Because AI rollouts rarely fail on the model—they fail on adoption. Shaping workflows that people actually want to use has been my work for more than 15 years. Many AI leads today come from product, operations, and business functions, not from research.

Why not a large consultancy?

I build myself, work directly with your teams, and hand over to your people. For specialist topics such as legal, IT security, or complex data architecture, I bring in the right partners.

How do you handle the works council and data protection?

Early and openly. The works council, data protection, and IT security are involved from the start; a clear AI guideline and data rules are among the first outcomes. Where AI prepares texts or decisions, a person gives the final approval.

Do you know our industry?

Maybe not in detail—but the pattern, yes: viewer mail, donation letters, and funeral bookings work like quotes, complaints, or order handling. The expertise stays with your people; I make sure the workflow and tools fit it.

What does it cost?

It depends on the format. The intro call is free. After it, you receive a concrete proposal with scope, outcome, and price.

Interim or permanent?

Both are possible. A good path is often to start with an interim mandate and decide after 90 days whether and how to fill the role permanently.
