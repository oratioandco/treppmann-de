When to bring me in

When AI activity has not become a new capability.

The constraint is rarely just the model or tool. What is often missing is a clear priority, a better workflow, an owned product, and a practical path for people to use it.

Many ideas. No clear bet.

You have pilots and tool access, but no shared decision about which workflow to change first or how the organization will recognize value.

A consequential workflow is stuck.

Domain knowledge, exceptions, and handoffs do not fit off-the-shelf software. The team needs a better workflow and a system shaped around it.

Strategy and delivery are drifting apart.

Leadership, domain teams, product, and technology are working from different versions of the problem. Someone needs to connect the decisions and stay through adoption.
