AI adoption in practice

Bibel TV · Viewer services

A Copilot skill the team chose to use

At Bibel TV I built a Microsoft Copilot skill that drafts letters, emails and social replies for viewer services in the support handbook's house wording. Staff review each draft and keep every send decision.

I aligned with the department head first, made the trial optional, and ran a self-test week and one-to-one sessions. Complex letters now take minutes instead of hours, and temporary staff write in the house voice from day one.

The resistance was fear of the unknown and doubt that AI could ever sound human. Both holdouts became regular users. The first came round after the assistant answered a complex complaint letter, point by point, that would have taken hours.

Bibel TV · Product development

From agency dependency to building in-house

Development at Bibel TV used to depend almost entirely on an external agency. I showed what was possible in my own work and ran individual tutorial sessions.

Together with the team we introduced Claude Code, GitHub with Spec Kit, MCP servers for internal documentation and shared rules in the company Claude account. Far more is now built in-house by product managers and product owners, including most of the backend. One or two internal champions now carry it on.
